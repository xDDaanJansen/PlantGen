# Make a hanging raceme

For a spike of flowers ranged down one side and pointing down, like a foxglove,
or for racemes that hang from the stem as a whole, like laburnum. The numbers
below are measured on the Generic perennial at Day 270.

## Turn the inflorescence into a raceme

![A raceme flower component's settings](components-flower)

1. Select the flower component in **Components**.
2. Under **Inflorescence**, set **Type** to **Raceme**.

**Count** sets the flowers per raceme and **Spacing** how close they sit, as a
fraction of the stem's own internode length.

## Crowd the flowers onto one side

Lower **Spread**, the share of the stem's circumference the flowers occupy. At
360 the seven flowers of the main raceme spread over 275 degrees; at 180 over
154 degrees; at 100 they gather onto 85 degrees, one side of the spire.

## Point the flowers down

Set the pedicel's **Angle** negative. 0 holds each pedicel straight out, positive
angles it up toward the tip, and negative hangs it: at -70 every pedicel hangs
at -70 degrees. The angle works whatever the flower weighs.

**Bend** curves each pedicel without moving its flower.

## Hang the whole raceme from the stem

For racemes that leave the stem at the nodes and hang, laburnum style:

1. Set **Attachment Location** to **Axillary**.
2. Set **Orientation** to **Pendulous** and raise **Strength**.

With no pull the racemes leave the stem at 86 degrees from vertical. At
Strength 0.5 they hang to 128 degrees, at 1 to 150 degrees. The stem's own
weight bends them a little further, so even at 1 they are not forced to
exactly vertical.

**Reach It As Flowers Open** makes a raceme arrive at the pendulous direction as
it fills with flowers, upright while it is in bud and bowed over once laden.

## Open the flowers from the bottom

**Order** under **Bloom** sets which flowers open first. **Bottom first** opens
the raceme from its base to its tip, as a foxglove does.

Model the bell or flower mesh yourself, or build it with the Flower Generator.
