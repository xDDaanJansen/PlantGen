# Prune limbs by hand

For cutting a branch off the grown plant, the way a gardener would, without
changing anything else about how it grows.

1. Open **Physiology › Pruning** and click **Prune Limbs**. The button becomes
   **Stop Pruning**, and the plant shows a labelled glyph at every node, bud,
   apex, leaf and flower.
2. Point at a glyph on the limb you want gone and press `X`. That limb goes,
   with every branch, leaf and flower growing from it.
3. Prune more limbs the same way, then click **Stop Pruning**.

The fold counts the pruned limbs. **Keep Dead Wood** leaves a pruned limb
standing as grey dead wood instead of removing it. It is off by default,
because a cut removes the limb. **Clear Pruning** brings every pruned limb back.

![Physiology › Pruning with two limbs pruned](pruning-fold)

A prune belongs to the plant's current branching. When a change makes the plant
branch differently, the pruned limbs no longer match, so PlantGen drops them,
the fold shows **Pruning cleared: topology changed**, and the plant grows whole
again. Prune last, once the branching is settled.
