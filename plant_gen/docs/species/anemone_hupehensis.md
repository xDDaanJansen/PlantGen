# *Anemone hupehensis* (Japanese anemone)

The spec sheet the bundled *Anemone hupehensis* preset is dialled to. Each value is
a range from the sources below; where they disagree, the Flora of China (the
primary botanical treatment) wins and the others are noted. Recently moved to
*Eriocapitella hupehensis*; the preset keeps the long-standing name.

## Habit

A clump-forming herbaceous perennial from a short, branched, woody caudex
(8-12 cm, spreading by it into colonies). A few long-stalked basal leaves; from
the clump rise tall, almost leafless flowering stems (scapes). At the top of
each, one whorl of three leaf-like involucral bracts, and above it a loose cyme
branching two or three times, every branch ending in a single flower on a long
pedicel. Flowers July to October. Garden forms (var. *japonica*) are often
double.

| Part | Value | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Scape (flowering stem) | 30-100 (-120) cm, sparsely hairy, leafless below the bracts | FoC |
| Height, garden plants | 0.5-1 m (to 1.2 m) | RHS; FoC |
| Caudex | short, branched, woody, 8-12 x 0.5-0.7 cm | FoC |
| Basal leaves | 3-5 per crown | FoC |
| Petiole | 5-35 cm, densely hairy, base sheathing | FoC |
| Leaf blade | ternate (three leaflets), sparsely bristly | FoC |
| Petiolules | 2-5 cm | FoC |
| Central leaflet | undivided, ovate to broadly ovate, 4-10 x 3-10 cm, margin toothed / shallowly lobed | FoC; PIER |
| Lateral leaflets | like the central one but smaller | FoC |

## The inflorescence

| Part | Value | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Type | cyme, 2- or 3-branched, many-flowered (a dichasium: each node forks in two, a flower at every tip) | FoC; Wikipedia |
| Involucral bracts | 3, in one whorl at the cyme's base; petiole 2-3 cm, base sheathing; blade like the leaves, ternate, 3-7 cm | FoC |
| Pedicel | 3-10 cm, hairy; the lateral ones with small paired bracteoles | FoC |
| Flowering | July-October, the central (first) flower opening first | FoC |

## The flower

| Part | Value | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Petals | none: the showy parts are petal-like sepals | FoC |
| Sepals | 5 (single flowers; 5-6 in wild plants), ca. 20 in double garden forms | FoC; Jungle Dragon |
| Sepal shape | obovate, 20-30 x 13-20 mm, 5-9 basal veins, velvety on the back | FoC |
| Colour | pink, purple-pink, purple-red or white; backs often darker | FoC; RHS |
| Flower diameter | 4-7 cm | Jungle Dragon; Gardenia |
| Stamens | many, 4-6 mm, a ring of yellow anthers round the centre | FoC |
| Pistils | more than 180, ca. 1.5 mm, in a green globose head at the centre | FoC |
| Fruit | a woolly ball of achenes (hairs 3-4 mm) | FoC |
