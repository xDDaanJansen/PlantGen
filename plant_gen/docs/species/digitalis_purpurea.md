# *Digitalis purpurea* (foxglove)

The spec sheet the bundled *Digitalis purpurea* preset is dialled to. The
preset is the user's own finished foxglove, brought to real size on
2026-10-25: it had been built at about a quarter scale (34 cm tall,
flowers ~1.7 cm), and every length was scaled x4, the flowers and leaves
to the sizes below.

## The plant

| Part | Value | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Habit | a biennial (sometimes short-lived perennial): a basal rosette the first year, the flowering stem the second | Sheffields; Flora of New Zealand |
| Flowering stem | simple, hairy, 1-2 m (to 2.5 m); the preset's spires 1.2-1.35 m | Sheffields; Flora of New Zealand; POWO |
| Basal leaves | ovate to lanceolate, 7.5-25 cm (garden plants to 35) x 2-12 cm, on stalks of 8.5-14 cm | POWO; Flora of New Zealand; Sheffields |
| Stem leaves | smaller up the stem, short-stalked to sessile | POWO |
| Raceme | one-sided (pedicels twisting the flowers to one side; cultivated forms all round), to ~60 cm long, many nodding flowers | POWO; Flora of New Zealand; Sheffields |
| Corolla | tubular-bell, purple (to white), spotted inside, 3.5-5 cm (tube ~4.5 cm) | Flora of New Zealand; Sydney eFlora; POWO |
| Bloom order | from the bottom of the spike up | (acropetal raceme) |

## The preset, as built

- Three spires from one rosette, 1.2-1.35 m, the racemes from ~72 cm up
  (~60 cm long), 30 flowers each; open corollas ~4.3 cm (the 4.3 cm bloom
  stage at Size 1.0).
- Basal rosette and stem leaves ~16 cm (the leaf meshes at Size 0.8), the
  basal ones on 12 cm stalks.
- Known gap: the leaf meshes are a cordate (heart-shaped) cut-out; a real
  foxglove leaf is ovate to lanceolate.
