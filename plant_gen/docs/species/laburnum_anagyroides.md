# *Laburnum anagyroides* (common laburnum, golden chain)

The spec sheet the bundled *Laburnum anagyroides* preset is dialled to. The
preset models a **young garden tree of about 4-5 m in full bloom, late May**
(user decision 2026-10-28): hanging yellow racemes among the young
trifoliate leaves. Pods and seeds are recorded below for reference; the
preset shows neither.

## The tree (what the preset follows)

| Part | Value | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Habit | a small tree or large shrub, often several-trunked or branching from the base; irregular branching | Trees and Shrubs Online; SAPS; NZ Flora |
| Height | 4-8 m (RHS), 5-7(-9) m (nursery), to 7 m (NZ Flora), rarely 12 m; the preset ~4.8 m | RHS; van den Berk; NZ Flora; Trees and Shrubs Online |
| Crown | open, vase-shaped; spreading branches, pendulous twigs | van den Berk; NC State Extension |

| Twigs | silvery-hairy at first, rounded, green for several years | NZ Flora; Trees and Shrubs Online |

## Where the racemes come from

No source found states it outright. NZ Flora calls the raceme axillary;
Oregon State calls the genus's racemes terminal; UHK puts the leaves on
short shoots, and for *L. caramanicum* Trees and Shrubs Online describes
short twigs each ending in a raceme. The preset follows what these agree
on: **each raceme ends a short, leafy spur from a bud of the previous
year's wood**, so the racemes hang along the branches, not at their tips.

## How the preset builds it (decisions, 2026-10-28)

| Choice | Value | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Size | ~4.8 m, crown ~3.9 x 4.1 m, an upright oval from ~2 m | the young garden tree; silhouette matched to the user's reference photos |
| Levels | Trunk -> Limbs -> Branches -> Spurs | long shoots monopodial; each spur sympodial, one unit that ends in its raceme (a pseudoterminal raceme on a short shoot) |
| Racemes | on the branches' spurs only, none on the trunk or limbs | user decision |
| Line ends | every trunk / limb / branch tip grows on as the next level down, holding that level's pitch from vertical | tip continuation |
| Flower | FlowerGen pea flower: the standard flared up, wings and keel forward (Around the Whorl curves), 18 mm; bloom stages turned so the standard faces the raceme's base | NZ Flora (15-20 mm) |
| Flower weight | 0.08 g | an estimate for an 18 mm pea flower -- no source found; the 0.4 g default bent every branch down |
| Bloom | Manual, Progress 0.7, Spread 0.8, acropetal: ~64% open, ~17% buds at the raceme tips | full bloom with the tips still opening |
| Leaves | trifoliate (Leaf Generator), leaflets 4-5.5 cm, four deformed variants; a rosette of 4 at each spur's base (Intersection) plus leaves along the young branches | NZ Flora; UHK (leaves on short shoots) |
| Textures | bark, twig, and one leaf + flower atlas (all four leaf variants and six bloom stages in one UV space) | user-made albedos |
| Seed | 11 | the most even crown of seeds 0-12 (scored on offset, evenness, outlier) |
