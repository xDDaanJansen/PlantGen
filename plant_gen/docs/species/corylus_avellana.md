# *Corylus avellana* (common hazel)

The spec sheet the bundled *Corylus avellana* preset is dialled to. The
preset models a **mature multi-stemmed shrub of about 4.4 m in late winter**
(user decision 2026-10-25): bare twigs hung with male catkins, before the
leaves. Leaves and nuts are recorded below for reference; the preset shows
neither.

## The shrub (what the preset follows)

| Part | Value | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Habit | a suckering, thicket-forming shrub of numerous upright stems from the base, more often than a single trunk | Jeremy Bartlett; havlis.cz; Woodland Trust |
| Height | usually 3-8 m (Burren flora: ~6 m), rarely to 15 m; the preset ~4.4 m (drafted toward 5 m) | Wikipedia; Burren flora; Woodland Trust |
| Bark | smooth, pale brown | Burren flora |
| Twigs | hairy | Burren flora |
| Winter buds | stalkless, short, rounded, greenish-brown | Burren flora |
| Male catkins | slender, pendulous, up to 8 cm (Wikipedia: 5-12 cm), hairy bracts, one flower of 8 stamens to a bract; green and tight through the winter, lengthening and turning pale yellow as they shed pollen, before the leaves | Burren flora; Wikipedia; Woodland Trust Nature's Calendar |
| Catkins together | hang in clusters; how many to a cluster is not stated by the sources found | Wikipedia (search summary) |
| Female flowers | stalkless, tiny and bud-like, only the bright red styles showing (1-3 mm) | Burren flora; Wikipedia |

## How it grows (architecture)

| Part | Value | Source |
|---|---|---|
| The stool | a basal-shooting shrub: new stems keep rising from buds at the collar / stool; the maturing stems lean outward, opening the middle, where young stems come up to fill it -- so a stool holds stems of every age, the young ones straight and upright, the old ones thicker, leaning and branched | Biological Flora of Britain and Ireland (Hicks 2022); PMC9787295 |
| Young rods | straight and fast -- coppice rods reach ~4-5 m in 7-10 years | Polyculture Project; Gardeners' World |
| Shoot growth | **sympodial**: the apical bud dies during the growing season and a bud below it carries the shoot on -- the twig bends at each node (a zigzag twig) | Tonda di Giffoni model (in silico Plants 2024); Green Seattle Partnership (C. cornuta) |
| Branching | basitonic over the whole plant (the vigorous shoots come from the base) but acrotonic along a shoot (its longest laterals near its end) | Tonda di Giffoni model |
| Along a year's shoot | blind (bare) nodes at the base, side shoots and flower buds in the middle, vegetative buds toward the end | Tonda di Giffoni model |
| Side shoots | mostly short: ~4 cm on average (0.6 cm near the base to 6.8 cm near the end of the bearer), longer on longer bearers; shoot classes short < 5 cm, medium 5-20, long 20-40, very long > 40 | Tonda di Giffoni model |
| Nodes | a 10-25 cm shoot has ~8 nodes; up to 0.43 chance of a same-year side shoot at a middle node | Tonda di Giffoni model |
| Catkins | at the tips of the same-year (sylleptic) side shoots, and at the nodes of the previous year's growth -- more on longer shoots | Tonda di Giffoni model; Oregon State thesis |
| Leaves (for the twig line) | alternate, in two ranks | Burren flora |

## Not shown (reference)

| Part | Value | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Leaves | alternate, deciduous, short-stalked, round to broadly oval, shortly pointed, softly hairy, double-toothed, 6-12 cm | Burren flora; Wikipedia |
