# *Araucaria araucana* (monkey puzzle)

The spec sheet the bundled *Araucaria araucana* preset is dialled to. The
preset models a **young tree of about 4 m**, roughly 20 years old (user
decision 2026-10-25: first a 1.8 m sapling, then grown on to 4 m), not the
30-40 m adult. It shows the species' signature -- tiers of five horizontal
branches, every surface armoured in overlapping scale leaves -- with the
leaves at their real size. Trunk thickening and the adult's umbrella crown
are out of scope.

## The young tree (what the preset follows)

| Part | Value | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Height | ~4 m (a tree of about 20 years) | Lusk & Le-Quesne 2000 (height growth 111-169 mm/yr) |
| Crown | broadly pyramidal to conical, the lowest tier the widest | Wikipedia |
| Branches | in whorls of five, from all round the trunk | KPU Plant DB; FloraFinder |
| Branchlets | on the older branches, in pairs in the branch's own (horizontal) plane: the branches are dorsiventral, Massart's model | Tomlinson 2009; the fossil *A. mirabilis* study (the Araucariaceae pattern) |
| Branch angle | horizontal or almost horizontal, the laterals horizontal or slightly pendant; the tips curl up (the "monkey's tail") | KPU; FloraFinder; Gymnosperm Database |
| Whorl spacing | one whorl every 2.1 years in the sun (2.6 in shade), so ~30 cm apart in the wild; closer in a garden | Lusk & Le-Quesne 2000 |
| Leaves | scale-like, thick, stiff, triangular / ovate-lanceolate, sharp-tipped | Gymnosperm Database; Wikipedia; RHS |
| Leaf size | 30-50 x 8-25 mm (most 3-5 x 1-2 cm) | Gymnosperm Database; KPU; FloraFinder |
| Leaf arrangement | spiralled and densely overlapping, covering every branch and the trunk | KPU; FloraFinder |
| Leaf life | 10-15 years (up to 24), so a young tree's trunk is still clothed | Gymnosperm Database; Wikipedia |
| Leaf colour | glossy dark green on both faces | Gymnosperm Database; RHS |
| Cones | none: a tree cones only from ~30-40 years | (adult only, below) |

## The adult (for reference, not modelled)

| Part | Value | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Height | 30-40 m (cultivated trees under 30 m) | Gymnosperm Database; Wikipedia |
| Trunk | 1-1.5 m across, bark to 15 cm thick, ringed with old branch scars | Wikipedia; Gymnosperm Database |
| Crown | umbrella-shaped, the lower branches shed | Wikipedia |
| Cones | mostly dioecious; pollen cones 7-15 x 5 cm; seed cones globose, 10-20 cm across, ~200 seeds | Gymnosperm Database; Wikipedia |
