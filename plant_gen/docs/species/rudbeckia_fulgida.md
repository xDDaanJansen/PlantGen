# *Rudbeckia fulgida* (orange coneflower)

The spec sheet the bundled *Rudbeckia fulgida* preset is dialled to. The preset
models the common garden form, var. *sullivantii* 'Goldsturm' (user decision
2026-10-25): the cultivar's own values first, the species' from the Flora of
North America (Urbatsch & Cox, the primary botanical treatment) where the
cultivar sources say nothing.

## The species

A clump-forming herbaceous perennial that spreads by stolons, each ending in a
new rosette, so a planting becomes a colony of rosettes. A persistent basal
rosette of long-stalked, coarse, dark green leaves; from it rise upright,
bristly-hairy, ridged stems, branching near the top into stiff, spreading
branches, each ending in a single daisy head held above the foliage. Flowers
late summer into autumn.

## The flower head

| Part | Value | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Heads | single at each branch tip, or 2-7 in a loose, flat-topped (corymb-like) array | FNA |
| Head diameter | 5-8 cm across the rays (to 12 cm in 'Goldsturm') | NCSU; RHS |
| Phyllaries | to ~2 cm x 2 mm, reflexed, bristly | FNA |
| Ray florets | 10-15 (7-20 in garden forms), orange-yellow, elliptic to oblanceolate, 15-25 x 3-6 mm, drooping toward the tips | FNA; NCSU |
| Disc | hemispheric to ovoid, 12-16 mm high x 10-18 mm across, blackish purple-brown | FNA |
| Disc florets | 50-500+, corollas 3-4.2 mm, yellowish green below, purple-brown above | FNA |
| Fruit | cypselae 2.2-4 mm, pappus a tiny crown (to 0.2 mm); heads stand over winter | FNA; NCSU |
| Flowering | (July) August to October | NCSU |

Wikipedia gives the ray laminae as 15-25 *cm*; that is a unit slip for the FNA's
15-25 mm.
