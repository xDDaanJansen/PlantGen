# *Lycopodium clavatum* (stag's-horn clubmoss, running clubmoss)

The spec sheet the bundled *Lycopodium clavatum* preset is dialled to. The
preset models **a patch about 1 m across: seven creeping runners from one
point, forking and rooting along the ground, with upright forking shoots and
erect, stalked spore cones, late summer** (user decisions 2026-10-28, widened
from one runner 2026-10-31 after the user's reference images). A clubmoss
is a lycophyte: no flowers or seeds; its "leaves" are microphylls (one vein
each) and its spores ripen in cones (strobili).

## The plant

| Part | Value | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Habit | a long creeping stem running on the surface or just under the litter, rooting at frequent intervals, with erect leafy shoots rising at fairly regular intervals | Minnesota Wildflowers; Wikipedia; Jungle Dragon |
| Runner length | to ~1 m (Wikipedia), to ~9 ft (Minnesota), creeping indefinitely | Wikipedia; Minnesota Wildflowers; Jungle Dragon |
| Upright shoots | to ~25 cm (10 in); fertile shoots 5-15 cm above ground | Minnesota Wildflowers; Wikipedia |
| Branching | each upright shoot with 3-6 ascending to spreading branches, the main branches with a few laterals; lycopod branching is dichotomous at heart (a shoot apex splits), often unequal | Minnesota Wildflowers; UBC Biology 210 |
| Yearly growth | each year's growth ends in a distinct constriction, usually with a whorl of smaller leaves | Minnesota Wildflowers |

## Leaves (microphylls)

| Part | Value | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Arrangement | crowded, spirally arranged, 12-20 to a cycle -- the stem looks 12-20-ranked and round | Minnesota Wildflowers |
| Size | 4-6 mm long, to 0.8 mm wide (Wikipedia: 3-5 x 0.7-1 mm) | Minnesota Wildflowers; Wikipedia |
| Shape | linear, untoothed, spreading to ascending, often curved upward | Minnesota Wildflowers |
| Tip | a white hair-like extension half or more as long as the blade; usually persists | Minnesota Wildflowers; Wikipedia |
| Colour | medium green, evergreen | Minnesota Wildflowers |

## Cones (strobili)

| Part | Value | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Peduncle | to ~13 cm (5 in) (Pennsylvania: 8-15 cm), with appressed, scale-like, hair-tipped leaves spaced along it | Minnesota Wildflowers; PA Ferns |
| Cones per peduncle | 2-5, rarely 1, each on its own short stalk | Minnesota Wildflowers |
| Cone size | 1.3-3.2 cm long (Minnesota), 2-3 x ~0.5 cm (Wikipedia), 15-55 x 3-6 mm (key) | Minnesota Wildflowers; Wikipedia; Go Botany |
| Sporophylls | to 2.5 mm, teardrop-shaped, sharp-pointed with a hair-like tip | Minnesota Wildflowers |
| Colour / timing | light green and tight when young, yellowish as they mature, light brown when dry; spores late summer into autumn; cones persist through winter | Minnesota Wildflowers |
